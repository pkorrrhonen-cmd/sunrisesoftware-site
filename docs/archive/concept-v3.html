About
Petri Korhonen
Tampere, Finland
Full-stack developer Physiotherapist, 20 years Development manager Data Protection Officer Extreme overclocker, SF3D

Twenty years of physiotherapy taught me to solve problems practically, not theoretically. Extreme overclocking taught me to push systems beyond their specifications. Now I do both with software.

I write real-time DSP that runs in the browser. I build AI research platforms that challenge their own conclusions. I design health trackers I actually use on my bike. Every product exists because the alternative was missing, broken, or built by someone who didn't understand the problem.
