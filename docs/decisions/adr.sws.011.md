## Context

Round 2 (`docs/mockups/flow-1.html`, captures `flow-1-desktop.png` and `flow-1-phone.png`)
put the whole page into palette A′ with the motion system: the sky layer whose light moves
with scroll, the drifting hero glow and the cursor light, the feathered portrait, the nav sun
rising with scroll, word reveals, parallax plates, a living Atlas plate, the cabinet as drawers
(hover pulls, click opens, the strip is dragged), the SF3D film strip, the rules, the creed and
the page dissolving into the sea. Type (DM Sans) and copy were placeholders on purpose.

## Decision

Flow 1 is exactly the right direction for the page. It is polished together from 18.9.2026
onwards with Petri's detailed answers to the round-2 questions (amount of motion, the drawer
metaphor, the sea, the nav sun, missing elements). After the polish comes the typeface round,
then texts and content, then SEED v3.0.0, and only then the page itself.

## Consequences

The mockup is the reference from here: changes are made to `flow-1.html` (or its successor
`flow-2.html`) and looked at together, never to `index.html` until the SEED is accepted.
Placeholder decisions still open: the typeface (Fraunces rejected), the creed's wording, the
SF3D placement (adr.sws.003), the Atlas plate's contrast, real images for the drawers that
still carry typographic cards.
