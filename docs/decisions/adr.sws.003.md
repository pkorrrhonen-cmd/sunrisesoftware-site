## Context

V4.5 gives the 2006 to 2016 overclocking archive a full section with ten photographs, an ember
accent and two venture stories, larger than any product on the page, and the opening headline
leads with it. By September 2026 the portfolio has grown (Atlas, Virustutka, Ultra Gravel,
ReviewGlass, Salpa Defence, Somnus) and the heritage also has its own home at sf3d.fi (SF3D AI
Lab, "from world records to world models"). Petri's instruction on 17.9.2026: push the history
further into the background, as a small side note. His correction the same evening: this is
not a request to remove it; its role and visibility are to be redesigned, calmly and from
mockups. A session's first draft had read the instruction as removal and proposed deleting the
unused photographs; that reading is withdrawn.

## Decision

Open. The SF3D history stays on the page with a smaller footprint and a later position than
in V4.5, and the years read 2006 to 2016. The shape is chosen from two or three mockups
(SEED v2.1.0 section 4.4 lists the alternatives: a footnote, a compact strip with a few images,
a shortened section after Approach). No image leaves `images/` before the choice; the photos
that the chosen shape does not use are decided then. Ember stays with SF3D wherever it lands.

## Consequences

The page's hierarchy changes in favour of the present work, and the history keeps its place
as part of the story. The decision file is completed with the chosen shape, the date and the
mockup it came from.
