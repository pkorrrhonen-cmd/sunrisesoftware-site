## Context

The 2026 luxury and craft market is rejecting minimalist sans-serif "blanding" for heritage
serifs and explained process. The story angle is synthesis across fields, and the earlier
SaaS-style hero plus product grid (SEED v1.1.0) read as an agency template.

## Decision

The page mirrors a research monograph or museum catalogue: numbered section marks, Fraunces
display serif, the cabinet-dark palette (warm black, paper text, brass accent). First-person
singular voice. No hero statistics, no feature proof, no generic AI language.

## Consequences

Authority through restraint and substance rather than adjectives; the visual quality itself is
the proof of capability. Copy and design need discipline: the direction is locked and a change
to it needs a major SEED version. Content inside the direction changes with a minor version
(SEED v2.1.0, September 2026).
