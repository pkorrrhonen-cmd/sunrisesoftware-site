# Look brief: sunrise over the sea (September 2026)

Petri's direction, 17.9.2026: the cabinet-dark theme of V4.5 has become the default look of AI
sites in 2026. Luviamo's branding is the family's best and does not read as a language-model
product. The site should be designed with the Sunrise idea: a warm palette with a breath of the
sunrise over the sea, the first rays hitting the eyes and the warmth spreading over the face,
carried the way Luviamo carries its birch forest: as atmosphere, not as pictures. Once the look
holds, placing the other projects on the page gets easier.

A change of direction is a major SEED version (v2.0.0 §0 rule). Nothing is decided before mockup
images have been looked at together; the page is edited only together after that (CLAUDE.md).

## What Luviamo actually does (read 17.9.2026 from LUVIAMO-SEED §10 to §11 and site tokens)

- **Philosophy** (§10.1): a Finnish birch forest in spring, golden sunlight through morning mist,
  calm and clarity; "one should feel like breathing in deeply". Nature meets technology; mist
  that thins and reveals clarity.
- **Palette**: a light base (Valo `#f0f7ec`, Usva `#f8faf6`), text in deep forest green
  (`#2d4430`, Metsä `#1a2e1a`), one warm accent family (Kulta `#d4a96a`, Kulta-d `#a67a3c`,
  Tuohi `#ecd9b8`, Oranssi `#c47d2e` for the sun mark). The dark greens appear only in the nav,
  the footer and one deep section band; the page reads light.
- **Typography**: Fraunces 300 to 500 for headings, upright only (italics were removed for
  readability), DM Sans for everything else, Bodoni Moda only in the wordmark whose i-dot is a
  sun with rays.
- **Atmosphere devices** (§11.3): four blurred golden light orbs drifting slowly in the hero at
  8 to 14 percent opacity, a soft vignette, cards with pastel gradients and a three-layer shadow,
  a featured card in deep gold, rounded CTAs, scroll reveal at 0.65 s with 80 ms stagger.
- **What it is not**: no photographs of forests, no leaf icons, no green-on-green. The forest is
  in the temperature of the page.

## Transfer to Sunrise: the sunrise as atmosphere

The same method with a different morning. The birch forest becomes the sea horizon at dawn:

- **Base**: warm paper, not white and not dark. The light of the page is the light before the sun
  is fully up.
- **Horizon**: one soft vertical gradient in the hero, warm at the top (sky just lit), cooler at
  the bottom (the sea still in shadow), and one large radial glow near the horizon line. This is
  the "first rays" device, used once, at the top; the rest of the page is calm paper.
- **Sun**: one accent family (gold to orange-brown), used the way Luviamo uses Kulta: the mark,
  the emphasised words, links, the live dot. The wordmark can carry the Sunrise sun the way
  Luviamo's i carries its sun (a shared family gesture, drawn differently).
- **Sea**: a cool blue-grey secondary (the Oxide `#5B7A8E` that V4.5 reserved and never used) for
  meta text, rules and the footer band. The footer is the only deep band, as Luviamo's forest
  green is.
- **Type**: Fraunces upright for headings (family DNA; italics are an open question, see below),
  DM Sans body (family), JetBrains Mono kept only for the archival labels (section marks, years,
  statuses) so the cabinet identity survives the change of light.
- **Kept from V4.5**: the numbered sections, the archive rows, the restraint (no hero statistics,
  no adjectives), the lightbox, first person.
- **Portrait**: the studio portrait (`images/Petri.jpg`, light grey background) sits on warm paper
  with a multiply blend and a slight desaturation, so the grey studio background disappears into
  the page instead of floating as a white rectangle.

## Open questions the mockups should answer

1. Which of A, B, C is the right morning, or what mix.
2. Fraunces italics for the emphasised words (V4.5 signature) or upright only (Luviamo's rule).
3. Mono labels: keep for the archival rhythm, or drop for a fully DM Sans page.
4. The footer as a deep sea band, or a light footer with no dark surface at all.
5. The portrait in the hero (as mocked) or beside §01.
6. Whether the hero glow should move slowly (Luviamo's orbs) or stay still.

## Process

Round 1 (these three) is looked at together in the browser pane and as PNGs. Petri's answers to
the questions above shape round 2 (one variant developed through the whole page, desktop and
phone). When round 2 is accepted, SEED v3.0.0 records the design system and absorbs the v2.1.0
content plan, and only then is `index.html` edited together.

## Round 2: elements, motion, flow (concept)

One page, all sections, real motion, placeholder type (DM Sans everywhere, on purpose) and
placeholder copy from the v2.1.0 content plan. File `docs/mockups/flow-1.html`; `?static=1`
renders it without motion for the PNGs.

The story the motion tells: the page is one morning. At the top the first ray hits the eyes
(the hero glow, the warmth that follows the cursor). Scrolling down, the light spreads and
settles into clear morning paper (the work). At the bottom the page dissolves into the open sea
at dawn (contact and footer), wide and calm: build, then enjoy the world.

| Element | Motion | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Sky layer | a fixed layer behind everything; dawn light at the top fades with scroll, the sea rises in the last stretch | the light progression is the flow, not section boxes |
| Hero glow | three soft light orbs drifting on 18 to 28 s loops; a warm radial light that follows the cursor with lag | "warmth on the face" |
| Portrait | feathered mask into the paper, multiply blend, slow parallax | in the hero, edges gone |
| Nav sun | the sun mark rises above its horizon line as the page is scrolled: a sunrise as the progress indicator | one gesture that only this page has |
| Headlines | words rise in one by one on reveal | flow, not a fade |
| Product plates | the cover drifts inside its frame with scroll; hover lifts it and shows the open pill | quiet parallax |
| Atlas plate | connections flow (moving dashes), the model breathes | the schematic is alive, it is a control plane |
| Cabinet drawers | hover pulls the drawer out and a preview floats after the cursor; click opens the drawer with its facts and a strip you drag with the mouse | the galleries pulled out with the mouse, in the cabinet's own metaphor |
| SF3D strip | a film strip you drag, photos open in the lightbox (placement still open, adr.sws.003) | the archive as a drawer, not a section |
| Approach | rules draw their line as they appear; the ideology line stands alone | the page's ideology |
| Sea | the last section has no box: a long gradient into the sea with slow light on the water | the seamless footer |
| Reduced motion | everything visible, nothing moves | accessibility |

## Round 3 (18.9.2026): Petri's answers to Flow 1, and Flow 2

the portrait's top edge should fade better, so the page's tone can be tuned there; the hover
preview is good but twice the size, and it must stop following the pointer near the plus so the
plus is natural to press; the plus reacts to the pointer's approach by growing; interactions
should feel inviting and responsive. Motion: enough, but more animated: as you scroll, the mist
parts and the sun rises to light the part you look at; the rising sun in the mark is "ingenious",

## Type round (18.9.2026): six pairs on the same screen

Petri's constraint: not the ornate serif every LLM-made site now carries (Fraunces and its
family), and the page must not fall into the generic default grotesque either. The type has to
fit the sunrise: warm, calm, a little human, clearly authored. Six pairs, each with a different
character, shown on the identical Flow 2 hero and one section with placeholder copy so the choice
is about the letters. File `docs/mockups/type-1.html`; the pair is switched from a strip at the
top, and `?pair=N` opens one directly (for captures).

| # | Display | Body | Character | Why it might be the one | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Newsreader (light, optical size) | Albert Sans | a calm editorial serif, upright, low contrast, drawn for screens | warm without ornament; reads as a considered publication, not a template | serif at all: does it still feel "AI-site"? Newsreader is far from Fraunces in temperament, but it is a serif |
| 2 | Instrument Serif | Instrument Sans | a condensed, slightly quirky serif with a matching sans | distinctive at headline size, quiet in body; the pair was drawn together | the serif's narrowness can read as fashion-magazine; italics avoided |
| 3 | Bricolage Grotesque (light, wide optical size) | Figtree | a grotesque with real personality: irregular widths, warm curves | the "not generic" grotesque; carries the sunrise's friendliness without a serif | its quirks can distract at very large sizes; needs restraint in weight |
| 4 | Familjen Grotesk | Public Sans | a Nordic grotesque with open shapes and a soft, slightly wide stance | Finnish-adjacent quiet confidence; pairs with the family's Luviamo restraint | closest to the safe middle; could be judged "clean but forgettable" |
| 5 | Gloock (display only) | Golos Text | a high-contrast display serif used only at headline size, with a plain, sturdy body | the sun in the type: one bold gesture in the headlines, everything else calm | Gloock's contrast is dramatic; it either owns the page or fights it |
| 6 | Young Serif | Onest | a chunky, warm, slab-leaning serif that feels handmade, with a modern humanist sans | most "built by a person"; friendly, unlike anything on AI sites | at scale it can feel heavy; limited weights |

Mono labels: each pair is shown twice in the strip, once with JetBrains Mono labels (the
archival rhythm from V4.5) and once with the body sans in small caps, so the question "mono or
not" is answered in the same view.

What to look at: the headline's first impression, the italic-free emphasis (colour only), the
small mono or sans labels, the product name at h3 size, body text at 15 px, and the creed line.
